## What is this?

**6R** is a participatory voting experiment built on Ethereum. During EthCC, it's repurposed for what really counts: settling community debates on-chain.

Each attendee mints a Soulbound Passport, gets one vote, and casts it on the active proposal — live, transparent, permanent on Sepolia.

Can't make it to the vote yourself? Delegate to someone you trust. They carry your voice.

---

## How to participate

**1 — Get Sepolia ETH**
The app runs on Sepolia testnet. Grab some test ETH from a [faucet](https://sepoliafaucet.com) first.

**2 — Mint your Passport** → [/mint](https://6th-republic-dapp.vercel.app/mint)
Your on-chain identity. One per address, non-transferable. Pick a pseudo, a nationality, done.

**3 — Vote** → [/](https://6th-republic-dapp.vercel.app)
**YES** or **NO** on the active proposal. Final. On-chain. Forever.

**4 — Or delegate** → [/account](https://6th-republic-dapp.vercel.app/account)
Hand your vote to someone else. They vote, you both count. Revocable anytime before the vote starts.
